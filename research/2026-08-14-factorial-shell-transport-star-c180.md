## 0. Claim boundary

C177 leaves the same-curl reservoir realization gate. On the factorial
shell this gate has substantially more arithmetic room than the six old
\(A_2\) modes. Put

\[
 K=K_j=(j!)^8,\qquad q=(j+1)^8,\qquad J=O(\log(j+1)).
                                                               \tag{0.1}
\]

There are more than \(q\) integer wavevectors on
\(\lvert g\rvert^2=2K^2\) for all sufficiently large \(j\). A
reality-complete union of octahedral orbits can be chosen with

\[
                       Q=q+O(1).                          \tag{0.2}
\]

Unit positive-helicity vectors on this palette form an exact isotropic
tight frame. For every high source \(p\sim qK\), the linearized Euler
edge to \(p+g\) is transport-dominated. After one common reservoir
normalization, the \(Q\) forward edges form an
\(O(J^{-1}+q^{-1})\)-isometry on the full two-dimensional polarization
fiber \(p^\perp\), uniformly on a narrowed C176 slab. A constant-degree
conflict graph removes every equality \(p+g=p'+g'\) while retaining
\(q^2\) reality pairs.

These results do **not** give the required physical focus. The leading
star is the first Fourier layer of a transport/composition propagator.
Exact scalar transport by a divergence-free reservoir preserves every
\(L^r\) norm, including \(L^\infty\), even when its first Fourier
derivative has \(Q\) bright daughter edges. For the normalization below,
the reservoir strain over an order-one gate is only

\[
             O\!\left(\frac{\sqrt Q}{q}\right)
             =O(q^{-1/2}).                                \tag{0.3}
\]

Thus an instantaneous tight-frame Gram cannot be counted as pointwise
gain.

The surviving named obligation is the **finite-frequency composition
closure (FFCC)**:

> In the pump Lagrangian frame, identify the full divergence-free
> finite-frequency propagator generated by the same-shell palette as a
> bounded polarization transport composed with its volume-preserving
> flow, plus an error small in a norm controlling the coherent endpoint
> of all \(q^2Q\) channels; or exhibit and control a subprincipal
> stretching/Leray resonance which invalidates that description and
> yields the required physical focus.

If FFCC gives the expected composition estimate, this same-shell star is
a no-focus mechanism. If it fails, the controlled failure is the only
possible gain mechanism left in this branch. Nothing here proves FFCC, a
terminal concentration, a one-cell Navier--Stokes stage, or a
singularity.

## 1. The factorial shell has enough directions

Let \(r_2(m)\) be the number of ordered signed representations of \(m\)
as a sum of two squares. Every solution

\[
                         a^2+b^2=K^2                       \tag{1.1}
\]

gives the shell point \(g=(a,b,K)\), with
\(\lvert g\rvert^2=2K^2\). The sum-of-two-squares formula and
\(K^2=(j!)^{16}\) give exactly

\[
 r_2(K^2)
 =4\prod_{\substack{p\leq j\\p\equiv1\ ({\rm mod}\ 4)}}
                  \bigl(16v_p(j!)+1\bigr).                \tag{1.2}
\]

No prime-number asymptotic is needed. Fix the nine primes

\[
                    5,13,17,29,37,41,53,61,73.            \tag{1.3}
\]

They are all congruent to one modulo four. For \(j\geq2p\),
\(v_p(j!)\geq j/(2p)\). Hence

\[
 r_2(K^2)\geq
 \frac{4\,8^9}
 {5\cdot13\cdot17\cdot29\cdot37\cdot41\cdot53\cdot61\cdot73}
 j^9=:c_*j^9.                                             \tag{1.4}
\]

Since \(q=(j+1)^8\), this is larger than \(q\) for all sufficiently large
\(j\). No prime-number asymptotic is hidden in this comparison.

Let \(\mathfrak O\) be the signed-permutation group, of order 48, and
close the points above under \(\mathfrak O\). Add complete orbits until
the cardinality first reaches \(Q_0=q\). Every orbit has
at most 48 points, so the resulting palette \(\Gamma_K\) satisfies

\[
 \Gamma_K=-\Gamma_K,\qquad \lvert g\rvert^2=2K^2,\qquad
 Q_0\leq Q:=\lvert\Gamma_K\rvert\leq Q_0+47.              \tag{1.5}
\]

Assign a unit positive-helicity coefficient \(H_g\) to every \(g\):

\[
 g\cdot H_g=0,\qquad
 i\,g\times H_g=\sqrt2K H_g,\qquad
 H_{-g}=\overline{H_g}.                                   \tag{1.6}
\]

The reality-complete field supported on \(\Gamma_K\), together with the
old C121 positive-helicity pump on the same shell, lies in one curl
eigenspace. C177 therefore makes their sum an exact heat-decaying
unforced Navier--Stokes background. Heat multiplies all these modes by
one scalar and provides no time ordering.

## 2. Exact octahedral Gram and the full-polarization star

Signed-permutation invariance kills every off-diagonal second moment and
makes the diagonal moments equal. Taking the trace gives

\[
 \boxed{\displaystyle
 \sum_{g\in\Gamma_K}\widehat g\,\widehat g^{\,T}
                         =\frac Q3 I.}                    \tag{2.1}
\]

For real \(p\), a unit helical coefficient obeys

\[
 \lvert H_g\cdot p\rvert^2
 =\frac12\left(\lvert p\rvert^2
              -\lvert\widehat g\cdot p\rvert^2\right).
                                                               \tag{2.2}
\]

Consequently

\[
 \boxed{\displaystyle
 \sum_{g\in\Gamma_K}\lvert H_g\cdot p\rvert^2
                         =\frac Q3\lvert p\rvert^2.}      \tag{2.3}
\]

This identity is isotropic in \(p\); it is not a generic-frame or
probabilistic statement.

Let \(a\in p^\perp\), \(k=p+g\neq0\), and omit the common factor
\(-i\). The exact linearized Euler edge contributed by
\(H_ge^{ig\cdot x}\) is

\[
 A_{p,g}a=P_k\bigl\{(H_g\cdot p)a+(a\cdot g)H_g\bigr\}.
                                                               \tag{2.4}
\]

If \(\lvert p\rvert\geq cqK\), then
\(\lvert p+g\rvert\geq\lvert p\rvert/2\) for large \(q\), and

\[
 \boxed{
 A_{p,g}a=(H_g\cdot p)a+R_{p,g}a,\qquad
 \lvert R_{p,g}a\rvert\leq CK\lvert a\rvert.}             \tag{2.5}
\]

Indeed, the stretching term is \(O(K\lvert a\rvert)\), while

\[
 \lvert P_{p+g}a-a\rvert
 =\frac{\lvert(a\cdot g)(p+g)\rvert}{\lvert p+g\rvert^2}
 \leq\frac{CK}{\lvert p\rvert}\lvert a\rvert.             \tag{2.6}
\]

Thus transport is order \(qK\), whereas stretching and the moving Leray
fiber are order \(K\).

Use normalized inertial time \(\tau=a_jKt\), factor the pump velocity
scale \(a_j\), and let \(p_*=Kq\bar k\) be the central physical source.
Give every reservoir Fourier coefficient the common relative size

\[
       \rho_*=\frac{\sqrt3K}{\sqrt Q\,\lvert p_*\rvert}
                 \asymp\frac1{q\sqrt Q}.                  \tag{2.7}
\]

Define the collective normalized edge map

\[
 {\cal T}_pa=
 \left\{\frac{\rho_*}{K}P_{p+g}
 \bigl[(H_g\cdot p)a+(a\cdot g)H_g\bigr]
 \right\}_{g\in\Gamma_K}.                                \tag{2.8}
\]

Equations (2.3) and (2.5) imply

\[
 \left\|{\cal T}_p^*{\cal T}_p-I_{p^\perp}\right\|
 \leq C\left(
 \frac{\bigl|\lvert p\rvert-\lvert p_*\rvert\bigr|}
      {\lvert p_*\rvert}
 +\frac1q\right).                                        \tag{2.9}
\]

Thus the star carries both source polarizations; its principal map is a
scalar multiple of the identity on \(p^\perp\), not a one-helicity
rank-one edge. Equation (2.9) concerns a forward tangent row. It does not
prove depletion, a reverse row, or endpoint coherence.

## 3. A shrinking C176 slab retains \(q^2\) sources

C176 uses the orthonormal frame

\[
 e_3=\frac u{\lvert u\rvert},\qquad
 e_1=\frac{g_0}{\lvert g_0\rvert},\qquad
 e_2=e_3\times e_1,                                      \tag{3.1}
\]

where \(u\cdot g_0=0\) and the central ray
\(\bar k\in u^\perp\). Importantly, \(u\) is not planar:

\[
                    u=-T'(0)U_0-\sqrt2T(0)N.              \tag{3.2}
\]

Inside C176's fixed-aperture box take the narrower box

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 {\cal B}^{\sharp}_{q,J}=\{k:\;&
 \lvert e_1\cdot(k-q\bar k)\rvert\leq\delta q/J,\\
 &\lvert e_2\cdot(k-q\bar k)\rvert\leq\delta q/J,\\
 &\lvert e_3\cdot(k-q\bar k)\rvert\leq\delta q/J^2\}.
 \end{aligned}                                            \tag{3.3}
\]

The nearest-unit-cube erosion argument from C176 gives, independently of
the orientation,

\[
 \#\bigl(\mathbb Z^3\cap{\cal B}^{\sharp}_{q,J}\bigr)
                         \geq c_\delta\frac{q^3}{J^4}.     \tag{3.4}
\]

Indeed, eroding each half-width by \(\sqrt3/2\) gives an oriented box of
volume
\[
 8\left(\delta q/J-\sqrt3/2\right)^2
   \left(\delta q/J^2-\sqrt3/2\right),
\]
and the nearest-integer unit cubes centred at its points lie inside
\({\cal B}^{\sharp}_{q,J}\). This proves (3.4). Since
\(q/J^4\to\infty\), the count contains any fixed multiple of \(q^2\) for
large stages.

The C154 central-fiber return shear is

\[
                    M^m=I-mg_0\otimes u.                  \tag{3.5}
\]

Because \(u\cdot\bar k=0\), (3.3) gives
\(\lvert u\cdot k\rvert=O(q/J^2)\). Therefore, for
\(0\leq m\leq J\),

\[
                         \lvert M^mk-k\rvert=O(q/J).       \tag{3.6}
\]

Every carrier remains in an \(O(J^{-1})\) projective aperture about the
central ray, and

\[
 \frac{\bigl|\lvert Kk\rvert-\lvert p_*\rvert\bigr|}
      {\lvert p_*\rvert}=O(J^{-1}).                       \tag{3.7}
\]

Combining (3.7) with (2.9) gives the announced
\(O(J^{-1}+q^{-1})\) common-normalization error. This is a central-fiber
arithmetic statement. Stability under the base-dependent localized
covector flow and under the added reservoir remains part of FFCC/C125.

The reciprocal spatial bounding widths of (3.3) are

\[
                         Jr\times Jr\times J^2r.           \tag{3.8}
\]

A uniformly filled envelope has volume \(J^4r^3\) and an
\(L^2\)-to-point normalization tax \(J^2\). The full \(Q=q+O(1)\) palette
has no additional daughter-cardinality loss relative to C161's ideal
\(q\)-star. The \(J^2\) envelope tax is still only a kinematic ledger, not
a construction of a filled packet or a coherent focus; any use must pay
it in the endpoint normalization.

## 4. First-generation translation collisions cost only a constant

Use physical sources \(p=Kk\), with
\(k\in\mathbb Z^3\cap{\cal B}^{\sharp}_{q,J}\), and gates
\(g\in\Gamma_K\). A collision satisfies

\[
                   Kk+g=Kk'+g'.                           \tag{4.1}
\]

It follows that

\[
 d=k-k'=\frac{g'-g}{K}\in\mathbb Z^3.                    \tag{4.2}
\]

Since \(\lvert g_i\rvert\leq\sqrt2K\), every coordinate of \(d\)
belongs to \(\{-2,-1,0,1,2\}\). Thus the conflict graph on source
centres, joining two vertices whenever some collision is possible, has
degree at most

\[
                              5^3-1=124.                   \tag{4.3}
\]

A greedy independent set retains at least one vertex in every 125.
Start with \(125q^2\) candidates from (3.4), prune, and then add the
reality partners in the opposite box. Choose the fixed aperture
\(\delta<|\bar k|/4\). Every sum \(k+k'\), with \(k,k'\) in the positive
box, has norm at least \(q|\bar k|\) for all large stages, whereas every
conflict displacement in (4.2) has norm at most \(2\sqrt3\). Hence no new
edge joins the selected positive set to its negative copy. The resulting
\(q^2\) reality pairs satisfy

\[
 \boxed{
 (k,g)\longmapsto Kk+g\quad\hbox{is injective on }
 S\times\Gamma_K.}                                      \tag{4.4}
\]

This removes first-generation source--daughter collisions only.
Collisions with pump modes, reverse edges, higher sidebands, and the
retained wake are not covered by (4.4).

## 5. Exact principal-composition boundary

Let \(W(t,x)\) be any smooth divergence-free real field and let \(X\)
be its volume-preserving flow. The principal transport equation

\[
                      \partial_tf+W\cdot\nabla f=0         \tag{5.1}
\]

has the exact solution

\[
                      f(t)=f_0\circ X(t,\cdot)^{-1}.       \tag{5.2}
\]

Hence, component by component and for every \(1\leq r\leq\infty\),

\[
                    \lVert f(t)\rVert_{L^r}
                    =\lVert f_0\rVert_{L^r}.              \tag{5.3}
\]

In Fourier variables the infinitesimal transport convolution is
skew-adjoint in \(L^2\). Its first off-diagonal row is precisely the
leading \((H_g\cdot p)a\) part of (2.4). Iterating that bright first row
produces the higher sidebands required by the composition identity; one
may not keep the daughters and discard the cancellations.

For the common normalization (2.7), the crude coefficient sum gives

\[
 \frac{\lVert\nabla W_{\rm res}\rVert_\infty}{a_jK}
 \leq C\rho_*Q
 \leq C\frac{\sqrt Q}{q}
 =O(q^{-1/2}).                                           \tag{5.4}
\]

For the linearization about \(W_{\rm res}\) alone, the exact Euler energy
identity gives

\[
 \frac12\frac d{dt}\lVert v\rVert_2^2
 =-\int v_i v_j\,\partial_jW_i\,dx.                       \tag{5.5}
\]

Thus the reservoir-only tangent changes total \(L^2\) energy by only
\(\exp(O(q^{-1/2}))\) on an order-one normalized gate. This is
compatible with a nearly unitary Fourier star and does not itself exclude
redistribution into many modes. The \(A_2\) pump strain is not included
in this estimate; its Lagrangian conjugation is one of the FFCC
obligations.

Equations (5.2)--(5.5) do not prove a full velocity \(L^\infty\)
estimate. Stretching, the moving divergence-free fiber, Leray pressure,
conjugation by the \(A_2\) pump, finite packet width, and point
evaluation are all subprincipal but may not be discarded without a
uniform theorem. That uniform theorem is FFCC. Until it is proved or
explicitly violated, the factorial-shell tight frame is an exact
algebraic star and not a solve-worthy one-cell focus.
