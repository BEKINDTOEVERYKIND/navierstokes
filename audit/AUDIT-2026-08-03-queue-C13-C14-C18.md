## C14 — asymptotically finite active cells are impossible (return-cell-no-go-map §1)

Every analytic step re-derived:

1. **Rescaling (1.1).** `y=N_jx, τ=A_jN_j(t−t_j), u=A_jv_j` gives
   `∂_τv+B(v,v)=ε_j∆v+g_j`, `ε_j=νN_j/A_j`, `g_j=Pf/(A_j²N_j)` — exact
   (both nonlinear and time terms scale as `A_j²N_j`).
2. **Equicontinuity.** For `k∈S`, `|∂_τv̂_j(k)|≤|B̂|+ε_j|k|²|v̂_j|+|ĝ_j|`
   is bounded on the `ε_j→ε<∞` branch because `H^{s−1}` is an algebra for
   `s−1>3/2` — this is exactly why `s>5/2` is the sharp hypothesis. Note
   equicontinuity *fails* on the `ε_j→∞` branch, which is precisely why the
   note treats that branch separately; the structure is consistent.
3. **Compactness.** Arzelà–Ascoli on the finitely many coefficients, tail
   hypothesis upgrades to `v_j→v` in `C(I;H^s)`; `(I−P_S)v=0` so the limit
   genuinely has finite support.
4. **Bilinear estimate (1.3).** `B(v,v)−B(P_Sv,P_Sv)=B((I−P_S)v,v)+
   B(P_Sv,(I−P_S)v)` plus the `H^{s−1}` algebra gives the stated bound;
   hence `B(v_j,v_j)→B(v,v)` in `C(I;H^{s−1})`.
5. **Limit equation.** Integral form passes in `H^{s−2}` including at
   frequencies outside `S`; the limit solves (1.4) with finite support.
6. **Source theorem.** Kishimoto–Yoneda (arXiv 2110.08039) abstract
   confirmed by fetch 2026-08-03: "there is no 3D Euler flow with finitely
   many Fourier modes, except for stationary 2D-like flows and Beltrami
   flows", and the paper discusses the viscous (and Coriolis) case. Both
   exceptional classes have vanishing projected nonlinearity, giving (1.5)
   `B(v,v)≡0`. Paper-body check of the viscous extension still pending
   (same convention as C11/C45/C46).
7. **`ε_j→∞` branch.** Energy identity ⇒ `∫_I‖∇v_j‖²≤C/ε_j→0`; `0∉S` puts
   the mean in the tail; Poincaré on `v_j−v̄_j` gives `v_j→0` in
   spacetime `L²`, upgraded on interior intervals by interpolation with the
   uniform `H^s` bound. The stated initial-boundary-layer caveat is honest.
8. **Subsequence upgrade.** Standard sub-subsequence argument; the
   transfer functionals are continuous under `C(I;H^s)` convergence.
9. **Honest conditionality.** Lattice compatibility and the strong
   normalized bounds (1.2) are explicit hypotheses, and the note itself
   lists the five escapes; the nondegeneracy caveat (ratios can survive
   while numerator and denominator vanish) is correct and important.

**Verdict: VERIFIED-CROSS**, conditional exactly as stated in the note
(lattice compatibility; strong normalized compactness; KY paper-body check
pending). Load-bearing consequence stands: the gate extrapolation cannot
terminate in a fixed finite normalized cell.

**Verdict: upgrade ARITH-CHECKED → VERIFIED-CROSS** (derivations at the

## C18 — growing-ratio schedule (5.1)–(5.2), chain (5.3) (return-cell-no-go-map §5)

With `N_{j+1}=N_jr_j`, any per-stage ratio `r_j^x` telescopes to level
`N_j^x`. Using the C13 rows with `r_j=N_j^{1/32}`, `μ_j=r_j^{2+1/8}`,
`q_j=r_j^{1/4}` (the schedule's `ε=1/8, δ=1/4`):

```
E: q+2μ−5 → −1/2      D: q+μ−3 → −5/8 (×h_j=A_j²)      τ: −(2+ε) → −17/8
L³: q/3+μ−2 → +5/24    Re: μ−2 → +1/8
```

all matching (5.2) exactly. Independent amplitude cross-check:
`η_j=N_j^{−1/4} ⇒ V=N^{−11/4} ⇒ U=(E/V)^{1/2}=N^{9/8} ⇒ S=U/a=N^{17/8}`
(=1/τ ✓), `‖u‖₃=UV^{1/3}=N^{5/24}` ✓, `Re=Ua/ν=N^{1/8}` ✓.
The unlabeled **active heat error** is identified: it is the child-frequency
heat over the parent residence, `h_j·νN_{j+1}²/S_j = A_j²N_j^{2(1+1/32)−17/8}
= A_j²N_j^{−1/16}` ✓ — the freshly written child survives the parent's
active window. Chain (5.3) `A_{j+1}≪h_j≲L_j≪Q_j≪r_j`: the first
inequality is the (weak form of the) gain requirement against the
`e^{−A²}` seed, the middle is drift-per-cycle localization bookkeeping
(architecture-level, not derived in the note), and the forcing is sound:
bounded `r_j` would bound `A_{j+1}=log N_{j+1}`, contradicting `N_j→∞`;
hence `r_j→∞` and `μ_j=r_j^{2+ε}→∞` (5.4).

**Verdict: upgrade UNAUDITED → VERIFIED-CROSS** for the (5.1)–(5.2)
arithmetic (six of six exponents re-derived, including identifying the
undefined heat-error row) and the forcing logic; the chain premises (5.3)
are flagged architecture-level. The note's own conclusion — growing ratios
buy bookkeeping at the price of an unbounded-gain nonlinear return with
`O(N^{−∞})` shape accuracy, which is C19's open theorem — is endorsed.
