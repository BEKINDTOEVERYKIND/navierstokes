## 1. C193 attacks and repairs

Independent derivations attacked the stable-frame normalization, the
intermediate-energy factorization, the Dirichlet constants, reality and
continuous-time wording, reversibility, and the higher-jet proof.

1. **Physical frame normalization.**  The first draft applied the line-angle
   formula directly to the C159 coefficient slope \(x\).  The C159 frame is
   orthogonal but not orthonormal:
   \[
   |E_1|^2=D/|k_0|^2,\qquad |E_2|^2=D.
   \]
   The physical slope is \(y=x|k_0|\).  Using
   \[
   \frac{567}{20}<|k_0|^2<\frac{12123}{400},
   \qquad \frac{13}{100}<x<\frac15,
   \]
   gives \(1/2<y<2\), so the repaired physical projector bound is
   \(<5/4\) and the physical eigenbasis condition is \(<2\).  The filter
   now explicitly uses physical unit eigenvectors.
2. **Filter checker completeness.**  The first checker copied one sparse
   coefficient dictionary into the other and asserted the energy
   factorization only after a loop, at the trivial endpoint.  The repair
   independently expands
   \((x-1)(G^2-x)\), checks the identity at every diagnostic point, and
   reduces the all-\(n\) Dirichlet and gain claims to coefficient
   identities.  The exact overlap term is retained rather than assuming
   orthogonal profiles.
3. **Claim boundary.**  “No intermediate energy borrowing” was narrowed to
   no *discrete-return* overshoot.  An unsupported reality-completion
   sentence and an unproved Stokes-period obstruction were removed.  The
   result is explicitly a complex principal direct-sum lemma.
4. **Reversibility.**  The proof now tracks the actual C159
   \(\gamma\)-quadrature.  Central inversion makes \(\gamma'\)
   half-periodic and periodic return forces \(\gamma(1/2)=0\); the root
   reversor makes \(\gamma\) odd.  Substitution in the actual C159 frame
   yields the two stated identities for \(B\), hence
   \(M=A_{1/2}^2\) and \(A_{1/2}{\cal R}A_{1/2}={\cal R}\).
5. **Higher jets.**  The initial checker contained only scalar sentinels
   for \(D^2U,D^3U\).  The repaired checker encodes the exact modal output
   vectors, the weighted tight-frame identity, Cauchy and AM--GM
   factorizations, both cubic absolute-value branches, and equality
   witnesses.  It now mechanically proves the sharp constants
   \(3\sqrt6\) and \(9\).

After repair, three independent blocker-only reviews found no C193
overclaim.  In particular they confirmed the exact Dirichlet constants,
nonorthogonal overlap identity, \(>3000n^3\) clock, physical conditioning,
and central/off-ray boundary.
