\[

 Q>2S\Longleftrightarrow\gamma>1.
\]

For arbitrary equal-shape one-step schedules it also re-derived
\[
 g=q\rho,\qquad b=\rho q^{-1/2},\qquad 1<\rho<q^{1/2}.

global requirement is the exact energy-product sum (2.0a) in C188; the
displayed \(q=n^4,g=2q\) witness satisfies it with total energy at most
\(16/3\).

For the declared C176 upper-envelope rule it independently obtained

\[
 {C_{\rm col}(C_JQ\log n)^{7/2}n^{-Q}\over n^{-3S}}
 =C_{\rm col}(C_JQ\log n)^{7/2}n^{3S-Q},
\]

so fixed powers pass exactly when \(Q>3S\), giving the envelope infimum
\(7/6\).  It also checked, under the equal-shape ledger convention, that
no power ansatz is needed: with
\(K_*=C_{\rm col}C_J^{7/2}\) and
\(\omega=bq^{1/3}/[K_*^{1/3}(\log q)^{7/6}]\), the declared majorant
divided by \(b^3\) is exactly \(\omega^{-3}\).  Thus the full declared
one-step envelope has demand

\(\omega\to\infty\).  Approaching the polynomial floor additionally uses
