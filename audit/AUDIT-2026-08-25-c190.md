## 2. Independent coefficient and return check

At the origin,

\[
 M=J,\qquad D=R_t,\qquad g=R_te_1,
\]

so C183 gives

\[
                  p(t)=R_t(p_0-mt e_1).
\]

At a genuine coefficient return \(T=2\pi\), the horizontal return defect
is \(-mT e_1\), and hence exact return forces \(m=0\).  A projective return
does not evade this: the conserved nonzero vertical component would force
the projective multiplier to be one.

The intended C186 quarter episodes require a separate check because a
quarter is not a coefficient return.  Writing \(h=\pi/2\) and
\(p_0=(a,b)\),

\[
 |k(t)|^2=(a-mt)^2+b^2+m^2.
\]

C183's determinant identity makes both quarter maps elements of
\(SL(2,\mathbb R)\) only if the norms at \(0,h,2h\) agree.  Exact
subtraction gives

\[
 m h(mh-2a)=0,
 \qquad
 m h(3mh-2a)=0.
\]

For \(m h\ne0\), these require \(a=mh/2\) and \(a=3mh/2\)
simultaneously.  Therefore \(m=0\) is forced before the trace or box test
may be applied.  This prevents a determinant normalization from silently
hiding the affine covector drift.

## 3. Independent polarization check

For \(m=0\), take \(p_0=-e_2\) and the oriented orthonormal frame

\[
 E_1=n,qquad E_2={k\times n\over |p|}=-R_te_1.
\]

At \(t=0\), direct multiplication of the four exact matrices printed in
C190 gives

\[
 E_0^T\left(-A_0+2k_0k_0^TA_0\right)E_0
       -E_0^T\Omega E_0=E_{12}.
\]

Rotational covariance makes this identity time independent.  Hence an
episode of length \(s\) has exact map

\[
                         I+sE_{12}.
\]

In particular, the two quarter maps are both
\(I+(\pi/2)E_{12}\), and every full-return block is
\(S=I+2\pi E_{12}\).  The passive scalar has not disappeared: its
gradient supplies the nonzero shear coefficient.  What fails is flag
rotation, because the same coefficient appears in every episode.
For an arbitrary unit returning direction \(h_0\), the same calculation
gives \(B=\beta E_{12}\), where
\(\beta=e_1\cdot Jh_0\) and \(|\beta|\le1\); hence the common-flag and
trace-two conclusions exhaust the entire \(m=0\) branch.

## 4. Adversarial failure modes checked

1. **Planar-return unipotence was not substituted for Kelvin
   unipotence.**  The Kelvin connection was calculated directly, and only
   then was its square-zero property asserted.
2. **The vertical charge was not reset between episodes.**  It is a
   conserved Fourier charge.  Alternating \(m\) and \(-m\) would splice
   different cocycles and would require an unproved converter.  Reality
   pairing does not make them sequential maps of one fiber.
3. **The frame was not changed independently at the two endpoints.**  One
   periodic/co-rotating rule is used throughout.  A return-dependent gauge
   could manufacture different coordinate matrices, but it would make the
   box test gauge dependent and violate the common-frame pre-registration.
4. **Half- and quarter-period language was separated from genuine
   return.**  The quarters are treated through the determinant identity;
   the full \(2\pi\) return is treated as an endomorphism of the same
   physical fiber.  Both calculations give the same common flag.
5. **The two success alternatives were not weakened.**  Equality of the
   blocks contradicts membership in the disjoint ordered \(U,V\) boxes,
   while the exact product trace is \(2\), not \(2+\delta\).
6. **Finite-frequency scope was not smuggled in.**  With no positive
   principal margin, C190 claims no preserving error budget.  This is
   permitted by outcome (b), and it is not relabeled as a new analytic
   gate.
7. **Architecture scope remains correct.**  Another passive 2D3C orbit
   may have a different return law.  C190 is evidence toward the existing
   PPRG trigger, not the complete-class theorem required to fire it.
