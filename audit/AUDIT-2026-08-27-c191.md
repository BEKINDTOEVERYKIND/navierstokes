   has an upper scalar charge but no stage-action lower bound.

   a lower bound below the target is not an upper bound on the true
   cocycle.

   direction.

   validated interval enclosure, and is unnecessary.
6. **Use the held essential-radius form.**  Forbidden by the C189 citation
   restriction and unnecessary.

   band retention, active extraction, or coherent endpoint is proved.
8. **Treat the \(J^2\)-taxed historical target as the C191 pass
   threshold.**  The pre-registered test is the raw \(q^{3/8}\) deficit;
   no reduced alternative is credited.
9. **Infer universal secular lock from C190.**  False on the landed broad
   class: C159/C183/C184/C185 provides an \(m\ne0\) exact return and growth.
10. **Call the proposed two forms exhaustive.**  False on the current
   class: C185 is periodic/resonant, so it falsifies form (i) but does not
   satisfy the incommensurate form (ii).
11. **Fire the architecture trigger after a failed witness search.**
    Invalid: only a genuine class theorem can support a negative trigger.

## 6. Final scope

C191 supplies a positive explicit-constant estimate: the certified C185
floor has enough scalar exponent in a declared logarithmic window, and the
corresponding collar and pump-retention scalar powers are feasible.

Its negative physical verdict is narrower and decisive for the proposed
direct reconciliation: the needed actual stage coverage is not proved,
the growing vector is not identified on the finite stage band, bare scalar
\(L^2\) growth cancels under fixed-energy normalization, and C185 supplies
no C125 relative-return cancellation.

No third PPRG formulation is introduced.  The universal-lock form is
unavailable on the existing class, while the incommensurate transported-
frame witness remains the sole unresolved accepted target.  Failure to
find it is not converted into a new gate or a no-go.
