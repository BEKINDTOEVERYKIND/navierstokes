and viscosity law, the exact and approximate energy telescoping formulas,

the circulation maximum principle. The primary source cited for the latter

The audit independently verified the additional constant-one Fourier
interpolation estimate
\[
 D_n\le\sqrt{\mu_n}\int\|u_n(t)\|_{\mathcal X_{\mu_n}}^2dt.
\]

energies bounded above and away from zero. It does not exclude endpoint-only bounded

\(E_n\to E_*>0\). Nonconvergent energies bounded above and away from zero instead use the
separate growth contradiction; they are not assigned a dissipation limit.

identified with relative \(L^2\) error or weighted circulation error.

**Scope:** demand-side identities for the proposed full-state object.
Singular-viscosity graphs remain open. No general axisymmetric regularity
or Navier--Stokes singularity exclusion is claimed.
