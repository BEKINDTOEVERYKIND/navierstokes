## 3. Cell comparison proof

On every cell, interval evaluation gives a constant Metzler lower matrix
\(L_j\le B(s)\) entrywise. If \(\Phi_j(t,s)\) is the actual two-parameter
cell propagator, then

\[
 \Phi_j(t,0)-e^{L_jt}
 =\int_0^t\Phi_j(t,s)(B(s)-L_j)e^{L_js}\,ds\ge0
\]

entrywise because every factor is nonnegative. With

\[
 \alpha_j\ge\max(0,-L_{11},-L_{22}),\qquad
 C_j=L_j+\alpha_jI\ge0,
\]

the checker uses

\[

The certified \(\alpha_j<5\) and \(\Delta=1/2048\) keep the scalar factor
positive. Every subsequent operand is nonnegative, so directed-down
rounding and induction are legitimate.
